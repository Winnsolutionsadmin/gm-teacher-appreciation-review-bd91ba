Three rules

Teachers never ask

Only Jarred or Ashley ever mention Google to a family. Teachers never ask for a review, never ask to be named, never mention the jar.

Families are never told

They get a warm thank-you and a link. Never "your review buys the team lunch."

Yelp is never asked

Yelp reviews that arrive on their own count. We reply to every one and never solicit there.

How stars arrive

A star is any new 4 or 5 star review on Google or Yelp, from any family, new or alumni. One review, one star. Never taken back.

The ask is one warm email from Jarred, once per family per year: new families at 60-90 days, leaving families in their last week, everyone else on their enrollment anniversary. Same paragraph every time, one link, individually sent, Jarred approves each one. If reviews spike (3+ in a week), asks pause two weeks.

Friday takes five minutes: read the new reviews aloud, drop the stars, hand out whatever mark was hit. A hard review costs the team nothing; Jarred replies to the family within two days.
