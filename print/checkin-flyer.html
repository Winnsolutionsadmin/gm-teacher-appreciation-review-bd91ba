Teacher Appreciation Month

Help us fill the star jar

All month long we are celebrating the people who care for your child. In the staff room there is a big jar with twenty-five empty stars. Every review a family leaves puts one gold star in it, and as the stars add up, the whole team gets a celebration: coffee at the first star, Starbucks at three, lunch at five, all the way to a grand prize when the jar is full. If your family has had a good experience here, an honest Google review is the easiest way to add a star.
