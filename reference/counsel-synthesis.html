Sleepers (each named once unless noted)

  • C25 Red-Stone Ritual (glm + grok): the field chases positive sentiment; this makes the system provably story-triggered and removes staff fear of the mailbox.
  • C10 Founder's Seed Drop (codex): Jarred funds treat #1 with his own staff stories - proves appreciation is leadership-funded, not review-purchased.
  • C19 Wheel of Time prizes (gemini): time gifts (cover a class, leave 30 min early) beat cash for burnt-out ECE staff and cost ~$0.
  • C16 Gratitude-first reciprocity (claude): teachers write families FIRST; the only true zero-solicitation organic-review engine; no jar to demo so the field underrates it.
  • C30 Weird artifacts (kimi): child-height picture book, future-self postcards - tactile rituals that generate stories without anyone feeling solicited.

Traps (killed by the panel)

  • C3 Capitol Commute spine (codex + claude): timing asks to legislative rhythms breaks the steady-neutral-pace rail, narrows a universal campaign into a demographic gimmick. (The goodbye/alumni story ritual C23 inside it was NOT itself condemned - optional later.)
  • C5 Ripple Room (glm + grok + kimi, 3 kills): whole-team hour off is operationally infeasible for a 3-classroom infant/toddler center (staffing ratios), the visible chime token display leaks the reward mechanic to families, and negative-review ceremony risks performative shaming.
  • C18 Surprise-the-parents reverse wheel (gemini): delightful on paper but families would decode that their stories power a reward engine - destroys the firewall.
  • PF transparency paragraph: 6/6 called it a MISTAKE. Telling families "your story funds staff treats" is reward disclosure - transactional pressure, breaks the decoy metric, and produces exactly the incentive optics the platforms and FTC punish. Warmth does not make it safe.

Paragraph vote: PA wins 5/6 (glm voted PD)

PA (codex-authored): "At Growing Mindfully, we try to notice the small moments that shape a child's day. You can share privately with Ashley or Jarred, or leave an honest Google review if you would rather share publicly. Every story helps us listen, learn, and care for our community more thoughtfully."
