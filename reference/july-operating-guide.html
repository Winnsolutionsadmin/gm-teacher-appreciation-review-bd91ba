What Stayed With You / Community Moments - Operating Guide

SUPERSEDED 2026-09-02 by ../PROGRAM.md (Jarred: "it is just teacher appreciation"). Kept for the rails and rationale only.

Internal campaign OS. Family-facing name: nothing (families never see a campaign name; they see one warm invitation). Staff-facing name: Community Moments. Working title: What Stayed With You. Built 2026-07-23 from deep research (research/review-reward-campaign-20260723/report.md) and a 4-round 6-model counsel panel (SYNTHESIS.md). NEVER SERVED PUBLICLY: this directory is blocked by functions/_middleware.js except wheel.html (which discloses nothing).

The one-sentence design

Families share stories about their child's time at GM (any channel, any sentiment); the team gets celebrated on a guaranteed cadence with story-flavored treats; Google reviews grow as a byproduct through a separate, neutral, velocity-capped invitation track; Yelp is never asked.

The five rules (laminated card: print/cards.html)

  1. Treats are never tied to stars, review counts, or any platform. Stories choose the treat THEME, never WHETHER the team is treated. The treat floor is guaranteed.
  2. Only Jarred or Ashley ever send a review link or mention Google to a family. Teachers never do, and never mention treats to families. Jarred delivers the treats.
  3. Never ask anyone for a Yelp review. Yelp = respond to every review, keep photos fresh, nothing else.
  4. Never filter who gets the invitation by how happy they seem, never suggest wording, never ask that a teacher be named, never ask at pickup in person.
  5. A hard or critical story counts exactly the same as a glowing one (Red-Stone ritual).

Kill phrases (any of these spoken or written = freeze the ask track, tell Jarred)

  • "five stars" / "5-star" in any family-facing sentence
  • "spin the wheel for us" / any treat mention to a family
  • "mention Ms./Mr. [teacher]" / "say it was [teacher]"
  • "leave us a Yelp review"
  • any batch blast of review links

Roles

  • Jarred (owner): runs the engine. Sends cohort invitations, monitors review velocity, delivers treats, runs the monthly language-drift audit, responds to every Google review within 2 business days (Yelp too).
  • Ashley (director): collects and reads stories at the Friday huddle, runs the Red-Stone ritual, owns the lobby surface, flags kill-phrase near-misses.
  • Teachers: zero campaign duties. They keep doing the work families write about. They may send families handwritten milestone notes (gratitude-first: we thank first, always).

The weekly rhythm (Friday huddle, ~7 minutes)

  1. Ashley reads this week's Community Moments aloud (anonymized as needed; rating and channel never mentioned; a printed card per story goes into the jar).
  2. One spin per week MINIMUM (the floor), plus one bonus spin any week 3+ new stories arrived. Spin at wheel.html or the physical wheel. Prizes are team-wide or rotational (see below).
  3. If a hard story arrived: Red-Stone ritual FIRST (see below), then the normal spin. Never skip.

The wheel (campaign-noticing/wheel.html)

Team prizes only. Time gifts are rotational and covered one classroom at a time (never the whole team off the floor at once; Jarred or Ashley covers):

  • Starbucks run day (Jarred fetches the orders)
  • Ashley cooks lunch (theme picked from this month's stories)
  • Catered team lunch
  • Pastry + coffee morning
  • Jarred covers your classroom for an hour (rotates through teachers)
  • Leave 30 minutes early Friday (staggered through the team over the following week)
  • Team choice (the team picks any of the above)
  • Mystery treat (Jarred improvises something sweet) The wheel page intentionally says NOTHING about stories, reviews, or Google. If a family ever found it, it reads as a staff-appreciation spinner and discloses nothing.

Budget (fixed operations line, not performance pay)

$300/month baseline: one real team lunch ($150) + smaller treats ($150). Time gifts cost $0 and are the highest-value prizes. The floor spends regardless of story volume. Quarterly stretch (~$300-500) for something bigger is optional and never story-gated. Annual ceiling ~$4-5k.

The story channels (all count, all equal)

  • Brightwheel messages and replies
  • Email to admin@ or jarred@growingmindfully.org
  • Lobby story cards (print/lobby-card.html holds the invitation text)
  • Verbal to Ashley (she writes it down, tells the family "I'd love to add this to our moments" and asks permission)
  • Google reviews that arrive on their own (they enter the jar like everything else)

Consent staircase (ask once per story, in plain words)

Private (team only) | Anonymized (lobby wall / tour cards) | Named (website quotes) | Public (family posts it themselves wherever they like). Private is a first-class, equally-honored answer. One story never powers an invitation, a celebration, and marketing reuse in the same week; space downstream uses at least 30 days apart. Marketing reuse always gets a fresh opt-in via Brightwheel.

The invitation track (SEPARATE from the celebration track)

The only family-facing review language, used verbatim, is the approved paragraph (PA, in copy/staged-family-copy.md). Rules:

  • Every family eventually receives it; no sentiment filtering ever.
  • Cohort pacing: ~1 cohort per week, cohorts = enrollment-date anniversary buckets (metronome/cohort-metronome.sh prints this week's cohort from the mirror DB, read-only).
  • One ask per family per year maximum. A family that does not respond is not re-asked (12-month quiet rule).
  • CIRCUIT BREAKER: if 3+ new Google reviews appear within any 7 days, pause all outbound invitations for 14 days. Too fast is a WARNING here, not a win (Google filters velocity spikes at the profile level: review freezes, public warning labels).
  • Every send is drafted first and approved by Jarred before it goes out (standing GM comms gate).

The Red-Stone ritual (when a critical or 3-star review / hard story arrives)

Within the week, at the huddle: the story earns the SAME card into the jar. Ashley reads only the underlying need (not a performance of the text), the team takes one breath, names one thing that was already good, one small thing to try, assigns follow-up without blame. The scheduled treat proceeds unchanged. Jarred replies to the family personally with care. The classroom is never audited over a story; the process is.

The second loop (story reuse, all consent-gated)

  • Living Yearbook: anonymized story cards become the printed deck prospective parents draw from on tours.
  • Website: named-consent stories refresh the family-quotes section.
  • GBP: one story post per week max on the Google Business Profile (passive content, never a solicitation).
  • Ads: consented story lines may become ad captions later (separate approval; auto-pause if the circuit breaker is tripped).

Launch sequence (90 days)

  • Week 1: Staff onboarding (onboarding-script.md). Jarred hands out the FIRST treat before explaining anything. Kill-phrase card walked through. Founder's Seed Drop: Jarred puts the first 3 story cards in the jar himself, written by him, about the staff. First spin same week.
  • Week 2: Lobby surface appears (story cards + drop box). The PA paragraph goes out ONCE via Brightwheel/newsletter (after Jarred's send approval). Families only ever learn "GM collects small moments." No mechanics, no treats, no wheel.
  • Week 3+: Friday rhythm runs. Invitation track begins: first enrollment-anniversary cohort gets PA (approved send), ~1 cohort/week, breaker armed.
  • Week 6: First consented stories into the tour deck + first GBP story post.
  • Week 12: Language-drift audit (sample recent family-facing messages + lobby text against the kill-phrase list) + scorecard review.

Scorecard (90 days, Goodhart-safe)

Celebrate / track:

  • Stories collected across all channels (target: 30+; distribution across classrooms healthy)
  • Treat-floor honored rate (target: 100% of weeks)
  • Consent mix (healthy = real spread; all-public would be a smell)
  • Staff pulse: "I feel appreciated" monthly 1-5 (must not drop)
  • Response time to critical stories/reviews (< 2 business days)
  • Language-drift audit passed Guardrail only (never a target, visible to Jarred only): Google review velocity inside a steady band (roughly 1-2/week; 3+ in 7 days trips the breaker; long droughts just mean check the metronome, never push harder). Explicitly NOT tracked: star average, review totals, teacher mentions, Yelp anything.

What we never do (from the research, non-negotiable)

No incentives to reviewers. No sentiment filtering of who gets asked. No asking on Yelp. No staff review quotas or leaderboards. No asking reviews to name teachers. No telling families that stories fund staff treats (6/6 panel: reward disclosure = pressure + platform/FTC optics). No batch blasts. No staff or family members posting reviews without disclosed relationships. No licensing references in any copy. No em dashes in any copy.
