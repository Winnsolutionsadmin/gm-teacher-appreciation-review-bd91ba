Replying to reviews (public, Jarred only)

Every Google and Yelp review gets a reply within 2 business days, written fresh each time.

  • Thank the family by first name if they used it; name the moment they described, not the stars.
  • Never mention treats, the jar, the program, licensing, or other families.
  • Critical review: acknowledge the specific concern, say what changed or will, invite a direct conversation (phone or in person), keep it short. Never argue, never explain policy at length.
  • Never ask for a rating change, never ask anyone to leave another review.

Hard rules carried into every placement

  • No em dashes anywhere. No licensing references anywhere.
  • Never mention stars, ratings, treats, the wheel, or staff rewards.
  • Never send to a filtered "happy families" subset. Cohorts are enrollment-date buckets only.
  • Yelp is never linked, named, or hinted at in any family-facing text.
  • One ask per family per 12 months; non-responders are left in peace.
  • Every send: draft -> Jarred approves -> send. No automation ever sends on its own.
